- Fix checkout of a product whose texts use locales that are no longer in `LOCALES` (review Medium 3): the order snapshot keeps every real translation, the store read, cart and orders share one fallback chain (requested, default, first available), and a product with no title text is `409 checkout_refused` `product_title_missing` instead of a 500.
