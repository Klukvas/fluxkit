# API: email

The admin side of transactional email (ADR 0031 decision 15, plan step 9 of `docs/plans/2026-10-26-email.md`): delivery status, the test send, the outbox with detail, resend and cancel, and the template list with preview. The customer routes that cause mail (password reset, email verification) are in `customers.md`; the jobs that send are in `jobs.md`; the template extension point is `docs/contracts/email-templates.md`.

Every route needs the permission `email.manage` (owner and manager; the viewer gets `403`), reads included: the outbox shows recipient addresses, which are personal data. State-changing requests need `X-Fluxkit-CSRF: 1` and, with a body, `Content-Type: application/json` (otherwise `403`, before authentication). Every response carries `Cache-Control: no-store`. Problem bodies are the common `application/problem+json` of `catalog.md`. Rule codes of `rule_violation` are in the `rule` member. Nothing here returns or logs the provider key; addresses, variables, subjects and links are never logged.

With `EMAIL_PROVIDER=none` the status, the list, the detail, the cancel, the template list and the preview still work; the test send and the resend answer `409 rule_violation` `email_not_configured` (the same code the storefront never sees: its mail routes are `404`).

## A message

`{id, templateKey, templateVersion, locale, to, recipientKind, customerId, orderId, adminUserId, status, attempts, nextAttemptAt, leaseUntil, deadlineAt, providerCode, providerMessageId, lastErrorCode, resendOf, createdByAdminId, createdAt, sentAt, updatedAt, canCancel, canResend}`.

- `to` is the recipient address (personal data). `recipientKind` is `customer`, `guest` or `admin` (a test send).
- `status` is `pending`, `sending`, `sent` (accepted by the provider, not delivered), `failed`, `unknown` (the provider may or may not have sent it; never retried automatically) or `cancelled`. Codes (`lastErrorCode`, `providerCode`) are short codes, never provider text.
- `canCancel` is true while pending. `canResend` is true for a final mail that is not a `customer.*` account mail; it does not look at the provider or the template, the resend itself does.
- `variables` (detail only) is the stored template input: every value of a `customer.*` mail and any value under a key that names a token, secret, password, key or link is `[redacted]`; texts are cut to 200 characters.

## Decisions of step 9

- The test mail goes to the caller's own address only and takes the language of the request (`locale`, default `DEFAULT_LOCALE`): admins have no locale column.
- A resend of `admin.test` is allowed and goes to the resending admin's own address.
- The list pages by `limit` and `offset` like every admin list (the ADR sketched a cursor).
- The preview is a `GET` with `?locale` (the ADR sketched a `POST`): it reads nothing and writes nothing.
