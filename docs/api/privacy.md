# API: privacy (personal data export, requests)

Personal data of customers and guest buyers: the self-service export and account deletion of a signed-in customer and the admin Privacy area (lookup by email, privacy requests, export, erasure preview, erasure and rejection of a request). Privacy plan steps 6 and 7, ADR 0033 decisions 1, 6, 7, 9, 10 and 13, ADR 0035 (subject scope). Source of truth for routes and schemas: `docs/api/openapi.json`; the export document is the contract `docs/contracts/personal-data-export.md`. No feature flag: privacy is core. The retention job is job 12 (`docs/api/jobs.md`), off until `ORDER_CONTACT_RETENTION_DAYS` is set. ## Rules

- **Emails never travel in a URL.** The lookup and the request routes take the address in a POST body, so it cannot reach an edge proxy log (plan risk R2).
- **A request never stores the address.** `privacy_request` keeps an HMAC-SHA256 of the normalized email under `ORDER_TOKEN_SECRET` with the label `fluxkit:privacy-subject-email:v1:` (its third use, after the guest order token and the auth tokens). An export of an email subject therefore takes the address again in the body; a different address is `400 validation_failed` with `path: "email"`, `code: "subject_mismatch"`. Rotating `ORDER_TOKEN_SECRET` makes the hashes of open email requests unmatchable: reject and open them again.
- **Who the subject is (ADR 0035, core `privacySubjectScope`).** A customer subject (the self-service routes, or an admin request by account id) is the account; it also reaches the guest orders and guest mail of its address (rows that belong to no account) only when the address is verified (`emailVerified`): registration does not check the address, so an unverified account reaches its own rows only. An email subject (an admin request by address, identity checked off-band) is the guest data of the address and, if an account has that address, the account too. The address never reaches an order or mail row of ANOTHER account that carries it (a signed-in customer may type any email at checkout): that order is erased with its own account. One shared condition (`apps/api/src/privacy/subject.ts`) selects the subject's orders and mail rows for the lookup's mail count, the export, the preview, the erasure and its blockers.
- **The export** is `fluxkit.personal-data-export/v1`, JSON, `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="personal-data-<YYYY-MM-DD>.json"`, `Cache-Control: no-store`, read in one transaction. The service scans the finished document with core `findForbiddenExportKeys` and fails (500) rather than send a hash, a token or an internal id. Above 10 000 orders the answer is `409 rule_violation` with rule `export_too_large` and nothing changes. Titles of lines, adjustments and the shipping method are in the order's locale (fallback: `DEFAULT_LOCALE`); the cart's titles in `DEFAULT_LOCALE`. In v1 an adjustment without a recorded source shows `source: ""` and a cart line never priced shows `seenUnitAmount: 0`, `seenCurrency: ""` (TODO(fluxkit): format v2 may make them nullable).
- **Requests** are due 30 days after they are received (`dueAt`); `overdue` is true for an open request past its due time. Statuses: `received`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `rejected` (transitions from core). A closed request answers `409 rule_violation` with rule `request_closed`.
- **Audit:** the lookup (`privacy.lookup`, target the account, or the admin who looked when no account has the address), a new request (`privacy.request_changed`) and an export (`privacy.exported`) are recorded in the admin audit log in the same transaction (`docs/api/audit.md`); details are counts and codes only.
- **Rate limits** (in memory, per process): `dataExport` 5 per customer per 24 hours; `accountDelete` 5 per customer per hour; `privacyExport` 30 per admin user per hour; `429 too_many_requests` with `Retry-After`.

## Erasure (ADR 0033 decision 6; irreversible)

- **What happens, in one transaction:** the locks are taken in one order (the admin request row, then the customer row, then the subject's orders by id, then its mail rows); every status is re-read under them and core `decideErasure` decides. With a blocker nothing changes and the answer is `409 rule_violation` with rule `erasure_blocked`, the members `reasons` (`open_order`, `payment_in_progress`, `refund_in_progress`, `active_shipment`, `mail_sending`) and `blockers` (`[{orderNumber, reason}]`, `orderNumber` null for `mail_sending`). Otherwise: each order of the subject gets core `planOrderAnonymisation` (email, phone and names NULL, both addresses reduced to `{countryCode}`, `cancellation_reason` NULL, the guest link cleared, `request_fingerprint` replaced by a hash of random bytes, `contact_erased_at` set); every mail row of the subject (same scope) is deleted; the account is deleted (sessions, cart and auth tokens cascade; the orders' `customer_id` becomes NULL). Number, status, dates, amounts, lines, adjustments, the shipping snapshot, shipments, payments, refunds and every journal stay unchanged.
- **Blockers:** an order `awaiting_payment` or `paid`; a payment attempt `creating`, `awaiting_customer`, `processing` or `create_unknown`; a refund `requested`, `processing` or `unknown`; a shipment `pending`, `unknown`, `created`, `in_transit`, `arrived` or `returning`; a mail to the subject that a sender holds under a live lease (`mail_sending`: the lease is 2 minutes, try again after it; decision of plan step 7). Only orders and mail rows in the subject's scope count (ADR 0035 decision 3): another account's order, or a guest order with an unverified account's address, never blocks; a guest order `awaiting_payment` with a verified account's address does until it expires or is cancelled. No admin override in v1.
- **Not reached:** data already at processors, backups, edge proxy logs (runbook, plan step 11). The request's `result` keeps the erased order numbers and the customer id so an erasure can be re-applied after a restore.
- **Erased orders afterwards:** the order views show all four contact fields as `null`, `contactErasedAt` (admin) and no addresses (`null`; the database keeps the country); the order mail handler sends nothing (logged as no_recipient); a shipment create or refresh answers `409 rule_violation` with rule `contact_erased`.

## Store endpoint

| Endpoint | Auth | Purpose | Request | Response | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GET /store/account/data-export` | customer | Download the own personal data | none | `200` the export document (`subject.kind` `customer`); also records a completed self-service export request (no contact data) | `401`, `409 rule_violation` (`export_too_large`), `429` |
| `POST /store/account/delete` | customer, CSRF | Delete the own account now (irreversible) | `{password, confirm: true}` (strict) | `204`, the session cookie cleared; records a completed self-service erasure request with the result | `400` (missing or false `confirm`, unknown field; a wrong password is `path: "password"`, `code: "incorrect"` and counts as a failed sign-in, so the lockout applies), `401` (also for the second of two parallel deletions), `409 rule_violation` (`erasure_blocked`), `429` |

A guest token never exports: it opens one order, not a person's data. Guests ask the shop, which uses the admin routes below.

## Admin endpoints (all `privacy.manage`, owner only)

| Endpoint | Purpose | Request | Response | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `POST /admin/privacy/lookup` | What the shop holds for an email | `{email}` (strict) | `200 {customer \| null, guestOrders: [{id, number, status, placedAt, contactErased}], mailCount, openRequests: [request]}`; `customer` is `{id, email, firstName, lastName, isActive, createdAt, orderCount}`; `guestOrders` are the orders with this email that belong to no account | `400` |
| `GET /admin/privacy/requests` | List requests: open ones first by due date, then closed ones, newest first | `?limit` (1 to 100, default 50), `?offset`, `?status` | `200 {items: [request], total, limit, offset}` | `400` |
| `POST /admin/privacy/requests` | Record a request | `{kind: "export" \| "erasure", subject: {customerId} \| {email}, note?}` (strict; `note` 1 to 500 characters, trimmed: how the identity was checked, no personal data) | `201` request, `Location: /admin/privacy/requests/{id}` | `400`, `404` (unknown customer) |
| `GET /admin/privacy/requests/{id}` | One request | path `id` | `200` request | `400`, `404` |
| `POST /admin/privacy/requests/{id}/export` | Download the personal data of the request's subject | `{email?}` (strict; required for an email subject) | `200` the export document; an `export` request becomes `completed` with `result {orders, mail, sessions}`, an `erasure` request becomes `in_progress` | `400` (missing or different email), `404`, `409 rule_violation` (`request_closed`, `export_too_large`, `subject_not_found`: the account of a customer request no longer exists), `429` |

| `POST /admin/privacy/requests/{id}/erase-preview` | What an erasure would do now | `{email?}` (strict; required for an email subject) | `200 {accountFound, orders, ordersToAnonymise, mail, blockers, reasons}`; nothing changes | `400`, `404`, `409 rule_violation` (`request_closed`, `not_an_erasure_request`, `subject_not_found`) |
| `POST /admin/privacy/requests/{id}/erase` | Erase the subject (irreversible) | `{email?, confirm: true}` (strict) | `200` the request, `completed`, `result {ordersAnonymised, mailDeleted, customerDeleted, orderNumbers, customerId?}`; a repeat answers the same stored result (idempotent per request id) | `400`, `404`, `409 rule_violation` (`erasure_blocked`, `request_closed` for a rejected request, `not_an_erasure_request`, `subject_not_found`) |
| `POST /admin/privacy/requests/{id}/reject` | Close an open request | `{reasonCode, note?}` (strict; `reasonCode` a lower-case code such as `identity_not_confirmed`) | `200` the request, `rejected`, `result {rejectReason}` | `400`, `404`, `409 rule_violation` (`request_closed`) |

A request is `{id, kind, subjectKind, subjectCustomerId | null, channel: "self_service" | "admin", status, receivedAt, dueAt, completedAt | null, overdue, result | null, note | null, handledBy | null}`; `result` holds counts, codes and (after an erasure) erased order numbers, never contact data.
