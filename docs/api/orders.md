# API: checkout and orders

Placing an order (`POST /store/checkout`), the guest's own order (`/store/orders/*`), the signed-in customer's orders (`/store/account/orders*`) and the order domain service behind them (status transitions, payment, cancellation, expiry). Plan steps 5b and 6 of `docs/plans/2026-10-17-cart-and-orders.md`; decisions in `docs/adr/0015-cart-and-checkout.md`, `0017-orders.md`, `0018-scheduler-domain-events-and-ports.md`, `0019-orders-schema.md`, `0020-variants-with-order-lines-are-not-deleted.md`. Source of truth for routes and schemas: `docs/api/openapi.json`. No feature flag: orders are core. The admin order routes (`/admin/orders*`, plan step 6) are described under "Admin endpoints" below. Online payments (ADR 0032): the payment service, webhook, job 11 and the store and admin payment routes exist (`docs/api/payments.md`); the storefront pay step and return page exist (payments plan step 9, `apps/storefront/README.md`); the admin pages come in step 10. Without a payment provider an order stays `awaiting_payment` until an admin marks it paid or it expires. Shipping methods at checkout exist (`docs/api/shipping.md`, shipping plan step 6). The scheduler that expires orders is `docs/api/jobs.md`; the events and ports are `docs/contracts/events.md`.

Every route sends `Cache-Control: no-store` (errors included). Every `POST` needs `X-Fluxkit-CSRF: 1` and a JSON content type (otherwise `403 forbidden`, before anything else). Money is `{amount, currency}` in minor units of the shop currency (ADR 0011). Texts of an order are resolved to `?locale` (one of `LOCALES`, else `400`), by default the locale the order was placed in (or the default locale if that one is no longer configured), with the usual per-field fallback; the stored snapshot keeps every locale.

## Checkout

Body (strict; an unknown field is `400 unknown_field`; there is no field for a cart, a customer, a price or a total):

```
{ email?, phone, firstName, lastName, shippingAddress?, billingAddress?, locale?,
  shipping?: {methodCode, pickupPointRef?},
  expectedTotal: {amount, currency} }
```

- `email`: required for a guest, normalized like admin emails; a signed-in customer may omit it (the account email is used). Never verified. `phone`: optional leading `+`, 7 to 15 digits (spaces, dashes, dots and parentheses allowed), stored as `+digits` or `digits`. `firstName`, `lastName`: 1 to 100 characters. Addresses: `{countryCode (two upper-case letters), region?, city, postalCode?, line1, line2?, recipientFirstName?, recipientLastName?, recipientPhone?}`, `null` means not sent. All content rules are the core rules (`packages/core/src/orders/contact.ts`); every error is reported at once with its path (`shippingAddress.city`, `expectedTotal.amount`, ...).
- `shippingAddress` is required when any line's product type ships physical goods (`product_type.requires_shipping`); an order of digital goods only needs none (it is stored when sent). Checked after the cart is priced, so a changed cart (`cart_changed`) is answered first.
- `locale`: one of the configured locales, stored on the order; default `DEFAULT_LOCALE`.
- `shipping` (shipping plan step 6, ADR 0029 decision 4): the delivery method of `GET /store/shipping/options`. Required (`400 shipping_method_required`) when the cart ships goods AND the shop offers at least one method (active, in the shop currency, a pickup point method only with its provider loaded: the methods `GET /store/shipping/options` lists); refused (`400 shipping_not_applicable`) for a cart that ships nothing. **A shop with no offered method keeps the checkout described here unchanged** (no member, shipping 0). Every rule (kinds, pickup points, the address, `409 shipping_changed`, `503 shipping_provider_unavailable`, the snapshot and the shipping adjustment) is in `docs/api/shipping.md`, "The checkout's shipping member". This tightens the validation once a method is active (pre-1.0, `CHANGELOG.md`).
- `expectedTotal`: the total the shopper saw; with a method, the option's `total` (subtotal plus shipping), which the server computed. A **consent check, never a price**: the server prices the cart itself with core (`priceCart`, `buildOrderLine`, `computeOrderTotals`). A different total, or a line that is unavailable (product unpublished, variant off or without a shop price, or deleted meanwhile), answers `409 cart_changed` with `cart`, the cart exactly as `GET /store/cart` shows it now, and nothing is reserved.

One transaction, all or nothing, lock order **customer row (customers only, `FOR KEY SHARE`) -> cart row `FOR UPDATE` -> advisory lock on the guest email (guests only) -> product rows `FOR SHARE` -> variant rows `FOR SHARE` -> inventory level rows `FOR UPDATE`** (each pass sorted by id), then the inserts:

1. The cart is found by the session or the cookie and locked; two checkouts of one cart (two tabs, two devices) serialize, and the second sees an empty cart (`409 cart_empty`).
1a. **Open-order limit** (ADR 0024), after the replay check (below) and the empty-cart check, before any catalog lock: a customer, or a guest email (normalized, orders without a customer), that already holds `MAX_OPEN_ORDERS` orders in `awaiting_payment` (default 3) gets `409 too_many_open_orders`; nothing is reserved and the cart keeps its lines. A paid, cancelled, expired or fulfilled order does not count. A replay of an existing checkout is answered with its order even at the limit. The detail text is the same for a customer and a guest and names no order, number or address; a guest only meets it after sending that email in a checkout, so it reveals nothing the request itself did not carry. Race-safe: a customer's checkouts run one after the other under the cart row lock; guest checkouts of one email take `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on a hash of the normalized address, held to the commit. The limit counts emails and customers, never IP addresses.
2. The product and variant locks are taken BEFORE the catalog facts are read, so an unpublish, a price write, a variant switch-off or a variant delete either finished before the checkout reads (and is seen) or waits until the order is committed.
3. The cart is priced with the same pass the cart view uses (`CartPricingService.price`), the order lines are built from exactly those facts (full translated texts of the product, variant and option values, SKU, image URL, unit price, compare-at, quantity, line total, `requires_shipping`), and the totals come from `computeOrderTotals`. A line total or subtotal that does not fit is refused, never clamped: `409 checkout_refused` with `lines.N` `line_total_out_of_range`, or `409 rule_violation` `subtotal_out_of_range` (as the cart does). The texts are copied whole: every real translation of the product title, variant title and option names and values is kept, also in a locale that is no longer in `LOCALES`, and the default locale is not required (ADR 0025); a product with no title text in any locale is refused as `409 checkout_refused` with `lines.N` `product_title_missing`.
4. The order row is inserted with a random number `XXXX-XXXX` (a collision of the 40-bit number retries with a new one, at most 5 times, then the request fails loudly with `500`), then the stock is reserved at the default location, one reservation per tracked line, `reference` = the order number, expiring at the order's own expiry (`ORDER_PAYMENT_TTL_MINUTES`, default 30) plus a fixed grace of 10 minutes, so the order job always expires the order first. Every line is evaluated against the locked levels first; if any is refused nothing is written and `409 checkout_refused` lists EVERY refused line as `errors[] = {path: "lines.<position in the cart>", code}` with `insufficient_stock`, `variant_not_sellable`, `reserved_out_of_range`, `line_total_out_of_range` or `product_title_missing`. The body never carries a stock quantity. (`variant_not_sellable` here is a safety net: a line that is not for sale is answered as `cart_changed` before any reservation.) Untracked variants are not reserved and always pass.
5. The lines, the links to their reservations (`order_item_reservation`), the status journal row `(null -> awaiting_payment, actor customer or guest)` and the outbox row `order.placed` (payload `{orderId, orderNumber, status}`, never personal data) are written.
6. **The cart is emptied in the same transaction: its lines are deleted, the cart row and the cookie stay** (the shopper keeps shopping; a replay of the same key still finds the cart it was made from). A refused or failed checkout leaves the cart as it was.

A deadlock (`40P01`) is retried once by re-running the transaction; a second one answers `503 service_unavailable` with `Retry-After` and is logged by SQLSTATE only. The lock orders are designed so that it does not happen (the concurrency tests assert that no retry was needed).

### Idempotency

`Idempotency-Key` is required (a UUID, stored lower-case on the order, unique) and kept as long as the order. The order stores a SHA-256 **fingerprint** of the normalized request (email, phone, names, addresses, locale, `expectedTotal`, the `shipping` member) plus the id of the cart it was made from. Compared in constant time. Fingerprint version 2 (shipping plan step 6) added `shipping`: a replay of an order placed before that release answers `idempotency_key_reused`. A replay never prices again and never calls a shipping provider.

- Same key, same fingerprint, same principal and the cart still reachable: `200` with the same order (its current status). A guest gets the **SAME access token** as in the first answer (it is derived from the order id and the key, ADR 0023: nothing is rotated and both answers of a double submit work); the access window is renewed to `ORDER_GUEST_ACCESS_DAYS` from now. A replay changes nothing else: no new reservation, journal row or event, and the cart is not touched.
- Anything else with that key (another body, another cart, a lost cart cookie, a customer using a guest's key or the other way round) is the same `409 idempotency_key_reused`, which never says which part differed and never returns an order or a token. The cart id in the fingerprint is on purpose: only whoever can still reach the cart can obtain the token again.
- Parallel requests with the same key and the same body make ONE order (one `201`, the rest `200`); the same key from two different carts makes one order and the other gets `idempotency_key_reused` after its transaction was rolled back (nothing left behind); two different keys for one cart make one order and `cart_empty`.

### Guest access (ADR 0017, token derivation ADR 0023)

`accessToken` is 32 bytes (base64url): the HMAC-SHA256 of the order id and the idempotency key under `ORDER_TOKEN_SECRET`, so a replay returns the same value; only its SHA-256 is stored (`access_token_hash`) with `access_expires_at` (`ORDER_GUEST_ACCESS_DAYS`, default 90). The storefront keeps it in the confirmation link's URL **fragment**. The token opens exactly one order, to read it and to cancel it while it is `awaiting_payment`; it never changes contact data. The lookup and the guest cancel answer one identical `404` for an unknown or malformed number, a wrong, empty or expired token, the token of another order, and an order of a customer; the hash comparison runs for a missing order too and is constant time (`crypto.timingSafeEqual` on the hashes). The expiry instant itself is already expired. The hash never appears in a response, a log or an event; the token itself appears only in the answers of the checkout with that key (the `201` and its replays), never in a lookup, a log or an event. Changing `ORDER_TOKEN_SECRET` does not break tokens already handed out (lookup compares hashes); the next replay after the change derives a token under the new secret and the earlier one stops working. A customer's own orders have no token (session and owner filter).

## The order

```
{ number, status, placedAt, expiresAt, currency,
  items: [{sku, productTitle, variantTitle | null, optionValues: [{option, value}], imageUrl | null,
           unitPrice, compareAtAmount | null, quantity, lineTotal}],
  subtotal, shippingTotal, discountTotal, taxTotal, total,
  adjustments: [{kind, code, label, amount, includedInPrice}],
  contact: {email | null, phone | null, firstName | null, lastName | null}, shippingAddress | null, billingAddress | null,
  shipping: {methodCode, kind, title, providerCode | null, amount, payer, estimatedCarrierAmount | null,
             destination: {providerRef, number, kind, cityRef, cityName, description} | null} | null,
  shipments: [{providerCode, trackingNumber, status, createdAt, updatedAt, deliveredAt | null, cancelledAt | null}],
  history: [{status, at}],
  payment: {canPay, providers: [code], latest: {paymentId, status, failureCategory | null, expiresAt, redirectUrl | null} | null} }
```

`summary` (list) is `{number, status, placedAt, expiresAt, total, itemCount}` (`itemCount` = units over all lines). `shipping` is the immutable shipping snapshot of an order placed with a method (title in the read's locale; `destination` only for a pickup point; `payer` is the recipient for a method paid to the carrier on delivery, then `amount` is 0), `null` otherwise; its amount is also the one shipping adjustment and `shippingTotal`. `shipments` (shipping plan step 8, additive) are the carrier waybills of the order that have a number (a `pending`, `unknown` or refused one is left out), oldest first, with the normalized status (`docs/api/shipping.md`, Shipments); no carrier text, reference or parcel data; empty until the shop created a waybill. The only adjustment that exists is that shipping line, so `discountTotal` and `taxTotal` are 0 and `total` equals `subtotal` plus `shippingTotal`; with `PRICES_INCLUDE_TAX=false` no tax is added either (docs warn: tax is not charged until a tax plan exists). `payment` (payments plan step 8, additive; ADR 0032 decision 18): `canPay` says whether a new online attempt would be accepted now (core `decideNewPaymentAttempt`, the rule the create route applies under its lock), `providers` the enabled payment providers that take the order currency (empty when online payment is off or `STOREFRONT_PUBLIC_URL` is unset), `latest` the newest attempt in the shopper's terms (`docs/api/payments.md`, Store routes; `redirectUrl` only while `waiting`, to continue the payment), `null` when none was made. Never a provider status, code, id or card. Never in a response: ids of orders, items, products, variants, customers or reservations (the payment attempt id is the one id: the return page needs it), reservation or stock information, the acting admin, token hashes, the idempotency key, the fingerprint. The reads issue a fixed number of statements whatever the number of lines or orders (tested).

## Statuses and transitions (ADR 0017)

`awaiting_payment`, `paid`, `fulfilled`, `cancelled`, `expired`; `fulfilled`, `cancelled` and `expired` are terminal. The core table is the only authority (`decideTransition`); every change is a row of the append-only journal `order_status_change` and an outbox row (`order.paid`, `order.cancelled`, `order.expired`, `order.fulfilled`; no personal data).

| From | To | Who | Stock effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| (new) | `awaiting_payment` | checkout | reservations created |
| `awaiting_payment` | `paid` | admin or system (`markPaid`) | commit every reservation that is on hand; backordered ones are held 365 days |
| `awaiting_payment` | `cancelled` | the owner (customer, or guest with the token), admin | release every reservation |
| `awaiting_payment` | `expired` | system (`expireOne`) | release every reservation |
| `paid` | `fulfilled` | admin or system | commit the reservations still active; `awaiting_stock` while one is not on hand |
| `paid` | `cancelled` | admin only, with a reason | `release_unsold` (ADR 0022): still-`active` reservations (the held remainder of a backordered line) are released; `committed` ones are sold units and stay sold, restock is an inventory `return` |

## Order domain service (internal; the admin routes below call it)

`OrdersService` (`apps/api/src/orders/orders.service.ts`, exported globally; implements the core `OrderPaymentsPort`, injectable as `ORDER_PAYMENTS_PORT`). Each method opens one transaction, locks the order row `FOR UPDATE` FIRST, asks the state machine, applies the stock effect through the sorted batch helpers of `ReservationsService` (`docs/api/inventory.md`) inside a savepoint, writes the order, the journal row and the event, and retries a deadlock once.

| Method | Purpose and rules |
|---|---|
| `markPaid(orderId, {amount, providerReference, source}, actor?)` | The idempotent payment entry point. `order_not_found`; an `expired` or `cancelled` order is `order_not_payable` and nothing changes; on a `paid` or `fulfilled` order a different reference is `payment_reference_conflict`, the same reference is ok with `alreadyPaid: true` (and still checks the amount); the amount must equal the total in the order currency (`amount_mismatch`); a reservation that is no longer active (swept) is `order_not_payable`. The clock is not consulted: an order past its expiry that the job has not expired yet can still be paid. The reference is 1 to 255 characters (a caller bug throws `RangeError`) |
| `markFulfilled(orderId, actor)` | `paid -> fulfilled`; `awaiting_stock` (nothing changes) while a held backordered line is not on hand; `invalid_transition` otherwise |
| `markPaidIn(tx, orderId, {amount, providerReference, source}, actor?)` | `markPaid` inside the CALLER's transaction (ADR 0032 decision 7): the payments module applies a verified success so the payment attempt, its journal and the order commit together; same rules and answers; `markPaid` delegates to it |
| `markPaidManually(orderId, request, actor)` | The admin mark-paid route: the online-payment guard (below) under the order lock, then `markPaidIn`; `payment_in_progress` besides `markPaid`'s answers. The port's `markPaid` is unchanged |
| `cancelAsGuest(number, token)`, `cancelAsCustomer(customerId, number)` | Owner cancels; `not_found` as in the routes, `invalid_transition` unless `awaiting_payment`, `payment_in_progress` (guard below) |
| `cancelAsAdmin(orderId, adminId, reason)` | `awaiting_payment` or `paid`; reason 1 to 500 characters stored on the order and in the journal; sold units are not restocked, the still-`active` reservations of a paid order are released (ADR 0022); `payment_in_progress` for an `awaiting_payment` order (guard below) |
| `expireOne(orderId, now)` | Expires the order when it is `awaiting_payment` and `expires_at <= now` (`expired`, `deferred`, `not_due`, `not_found`); `deferred` while an online payment is in flight (10-minute steps, at most `placed_at + ORDER_PAYMENT_TTL_MINUTES + 30 minutes`, ADR 0032 decision 8; `docs/api/jobs.md`, Payment deferral); the scheduler calls it for every due order (`docs/api/jobs.md`) |

**Online-payment guard** (ADR 0032 decision 10, owner answer 3; core `decideOrderPaymentGuard`): a cancel of an `awaiting_payment` order (owner or admin) and a manual mark paid lock the order's payment attempts after the order row. While one is `processing`, `create_unknown` or `creating` they are refused `409 rule_violation payment_in_progress` and nothing changes. Otherwise every `awaiting_customer` attempt is closed `invalidated` in the same transaction (journaled with the admin, or as `system` for the owner); job 11 invalidates its invoice at the provider; a success that still lands is refunded automatically (`docs/api/payments.md`).

Races on one order have one winner: a payment, a cancellation and the expiry all lock the order row first (tested: payment against cancel, payment against expiry, parallel payments, crossing orders).

## Admin endpoints (plan step 6)

All need an admin session; the permission is the default pair of the controller (`@AdminController("orders", {read: "orders.read", write: "orders.write"})`): `GET` needs `orders.read`, every `POST` needs `orders.write`. Owner and manager hold both, the viewer neither (`403 forbidden`: orders hold personal data, ADR 0017). Anonymous callers get `401` (a customer cookie, a guest access token or any other credential is not an admin credential), a `POST` without `X-Fluxkit-CSRF: 1` gets `403` (a body also needs `Content-Type: application/json`). Every response is `no-store` and every error `application/problem+json`. The acting admin is read from the session, never from the request; it is recorded as `actor_admin_id` in the status journal and, for a payment, on the `sale` stock movements.

| Endpoint | Auth | Purpose | Request | Response | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GET /admin/orders` | `orders.read` | List orders, newest first | `?limit` (1 to 100, default 20), `?offset` (0 to 10 000), `?status` (one of the five), `?placedFrom` (inclusive) and `?placedTo` (exclusive): ISO 8601 with `Z` or an offset, between 2000-01-01 and 2200-01-01, `placedFrom` not after `placedTo`; `?q` (at most 254 characters, below); `?customerId` (the exact id of the customer account that placed the order, a UUID; guest orders never match; `400` when malformed). Filters combine (AND). A parameter that is not listed is `400` | `200 {items, total, limit, offset}`; item `{id, number, status, placedAt, expiresAt, total, currency, customerId, email, itemCount}` (`itemCount` = units over all lines; `customerId` is `null` for a guest and for a deleted account; `email` is `null` once the order's contact was erased) | `400` |
| `GET /admin/orders/{id}` | `orders.read` | The order in full (below) | none | `200` the admin order | `400` malformed id; `404` |
| `POST /admin/orders/{id}/cancel` | `orders.write` | Cancel an `awaiting_payment` order (its reservations are released) or, admin only, a `paid` one | `{reason}`: trimmed, 1 to 500 characters, no control characters other than tab and line breaks (strict) | `200` the admin order | `400`; `404`; `409 rule_violation` `invalid_transition` (already cancelled, expired or fulfilled), `payment_in_progress` (an online payment of the `awaiting_payment` order is in flight) |
| `POST /admin/orders/{id}/mark-paid` | `orders.write` | Record a payment received outside the system, through the same `OrdersService.markPaid` entry point a payment plugin uses | `{reference, amount: {amount, currency}}` (strict): `reference` trimmed, 1 to 255 characters, no control characters; `amount` an integer of minor units, 0 to 10^12, a supported currency | `200` the admin order. The same `reference` on an order that is already paid (or fulfilled) answers `200` with the order unchanged | `400`; `404`; `409 rule_violation` `order_not_payable` (expired or cancelled, or a reservation is no longer held), `amount_mismatch` (not the total, or another currency; also checked on an already paid order), `payment_reference_conflict` (already paid with another reference), `invalid_transition`, `payment_in_progress` (an online payment is `processing`, `create_unknown` or `creating`; an `awaiting_customer` one is closed instead and a later online success is refunded automatically) |
| `GET`, `POST /admin/orders/{id}/shipments`, `POST .../shipments/{shipmentId}/reconcile`, `.../cancel`, `.../refresh`, `GET .../label` | `orders.read` (the `GET`s), `orders.write` | The carrier waybills of the order (shipping plan step 8) | see `docs/api/shipping.md`, Shipments | | |
| `GET /admin/orders/{id}/payments`, `POST .../payments/{paymentId}/refunds`, `POST .../payments/{paymentId}/resync` | `orders.read`, `payments.refund` (refunds), `orders.write` (resync) | The online payment attempts of the order, refunds, a provider read now | see `docs/api/payments.md`, Admin routes | | |
| `POST /admin/orders/{id}/mark-fulfilled` | `orders.write` | Mark a paid order fulfilled | none | `200` the admin order | `400`; `404`; `409 rule_violation` `awaiting_stock` (a backordered line is not on hand yet; nothing changes), `invalid_transition` (not `paid`, also a second call) |

**`q`** is an order number (the way a person types it: lower case, dash optional, `O`, `I`, `L` read as `0`, `1`, `1`) OR an email (trimmed and lower-cased). Both are compared for EQUALITY: nothing matches by prefix, suffix or substring, `%` and `_` are plain characters, and an empty or blank `q` is no filter. The filters are ANDed. The order is `placed_at` descending, then `id` descending, so orders placed at the same instant keep their place from page to page. `total` counts the whole match.

```
  items: [{position, productId | null, variantId | null, sku, productTitle {locale: text}, variantTitle {locale: text} | null,
           optionValues: [{option {locale: text}, value {locale: text}}], imageUrl | null, unitPrice, compareAtAmount | null,
           quantity, lineTotal, requiresShipping, reservations: [{status, quantity}]}],
  subtotal, shippingTotal, discountTotal, taxTotal, total,
  adjustments: [{kind, code, label {locale: text}, amount, includedInPrice, source | null}],
  shipping: {methodId | null, methodCode, kind, providerCode | null, providerService | null, title {locale: text},

```

Texts are the full locale maps of the snapshot (an admin sees every translation). `shipping` is the order's shipping snapshot (shipping plan step 6; `methodId` becomes `null` once the method is deleted, the rest never changes), `null` for an order without a method. `shipments` (shipping plan step 8, additive) are every carrier waybill of the order, oldest first, each with its status journal, as `GET /admin/orders/{id}/shipments` lists them (the shape and the shipment routes are in `docs/api/shipping.md`, Shipments): no recipient name or phone, no carrier text. A shipment never changes the order: fulfilment stays this page's `mark-fulfilled`. `payments` (payments plan step 8, additive) are every online payment attempt of the order, oldest first, with refunds, journal and attention flag, as `GET /admin/orders/{id}/payments` lists them (`docs/api/payments.md`, Admin routes): no page URL, card system and last 4 only, no provider text. `isGuest` is true for an order placed without an account (an order whose customer account was deleted afterwards has `customerId: null` and `isGuest: false`). A line's `reservations` show the status (`active`, `committed`, `released`, `expired`) and the quantity only. Never in the view, in a list, in a plugin's hands or in a log: the guest access token or its hash, the idempotency key, the request fingerprint, reservation ids, locations and stock figures. The view is read in one repeatable-read, read-only transaction (a consistent snapshot) with a fixed number of statements whatever the number of lines (tested: 1 and 20 lines; the list issues the same number for 1 and 50 orders).

Rules of the actions (all through `OrdersService`, which locks the order row first, so a payment, a cancellation and the expiry job on one order have exactly one winner; tested with many rounds, also from two API instances):

- **Cancel of a paid order**: allowed for an admin only, with the reason; the sold units stay sold, NOTHING is restocked and NOTHING is refunded: record the stock `return` and the refund by hand (ADR 0017). The `committed` reservations stay `committed`, no `return` movement is written. A backordered line's remainder is still an `active` reservation (held 365 days since payment): the cancel releases it (`reserved` goes down, no journal row, no `on_hand` change; ADR 0022). An already expired or released one is left as it is.
- **Mark-paid** commits every reservation that is on hand (a `sale` movement each, `reference` = the order number, the admin as actor); a backordered line is committed up to what is on hand and the rest is held 365 days until `mark-fulfilled`. The amount is judged by core money validation first (`400` for a float, a negative, a too big amount or an unknown currency) and against the order afterwards (`409 amount_mismatch`).
- **Mark-fulfilled** commits the reservations still active (backordered lines) or answers `awaiting_stock` with everything rolled back.
- Every change writes one journal row (`from`, `to`, `actor_type = admin`, `actor_admin_id`, `reason`) and one outbox event (`order.cancelled`, `order.paid`, `order.fulfilled`; ids and the order number only). A refused action writes nothing.

## Problem codes of this step (additive)

Shipping (shipping plan step 6, `docs/api/shipping.md`): `400` `shipping_method_required`, `shipping_not_applicable`; `409` `shipping_changed` (`shipping` member); `503` `shipping_provider_unavailable` with `Retry-After`.

`409` `cart_empty`, `cart_changed` (`cart` member), `checkout_refused` (`errors[]` per line), `idempotency_key_reused`, `too_many_open_orders`; `503` `service_unavailable`; `rule_violation` rules `invalid_transition` (owner and admin cancel, mark-paid, mark-fulfilled), `payment_in_progress` (owner and admin cancel, mark-paid), `subtotal_out_of_range`, and for the admin routes `order_not_payable`, `amount_mismatch`, `payment_reference_conflict` and `awaiting_stock`.

## Limits and notes

- Stock location: the default location only, one reservation per line (ADR 0015). A shop with more locations sees `in_stock` but `insufficient_stock` at checkout until multi-location allocation is planned.
- The number of statements of a checkout grows with the number of lines (a lock, an update and an insert per tracked line inside `reserveMany`); the reads do not.
- Stock probing by repeated checkouts is bounded by the rate limits (`checkout` 10 per 10 minutes per address, single process) and the 999 cap.
- **Erased contact (migration 0009, ADR 0033 decision 5):** once an order's contact data is anonymised (privacy erasure or retention; nothing erases yet, privacy plan steps 7 and 8), all four `contact` fields are `null`, both addresses are `null` (the database keeps only the country) and the admin order's `contactErasedAt` holds the time; on a live order all four are set and `contactErasedAt` is `null`. The order mail handler sends no mail to an erased order (it logs the reason no_recipient; not a problem code). Erasure rules: `docs/api/privacy.md`.
- The order contact data is personal data. Logs, events and problem bodies carry none of it (tested by capturing the log output of a refused, a placed and an invalid checkout).
