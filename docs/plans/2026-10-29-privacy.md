## Goal
A shop built from the template can answer a data subject (customer, guest buyer, admin staff) and a supervisory authority: what personal data it holds, where, why, for how long and who else receives it. A signed-in customer downloads their data as machine-readable JSON and deletes their account themselves; the shop owner handles requests from guests and by email in an admin "Privacy" area (look up by email, export, erase), with a due date per request and a record that the request was handled. Orders stay as accounting records; their contact fields are anonymised on erasure and, once the owner sets a period, after it. Every admin action on personal data leaves a minimal audit trail. The storefront has privacy notice, terms and cookie pages as replaceable i18n placeholders, sets only strictly necessary cookies, and a set of guard tests keeps personal data out of logs, event payloads and error codes and makes every new database column declare what it holds.

## Scope (proposed v1; the owner fixes it with questions 1 to 4)
**In v1:**
- A data inventory in code (`apps/api/src/privacy/inventory/`): every table and column classified (personal, pseudonymous, staff, secret hash, none), with retention, export and erasure action; a guard spec fails on an unclassified column. The human-readable copy in `docs/privacy/data-inventory.md`.
- Pure privacy rules in core (`packages/core/src/privacy/`): erasure eligibility (blockers), the anonymisation plan as data, the export document shape and version, retention cut-offs, request statuses and due dates.
- Migration (next free number, expected `0009_privacy`): erased-contact representation on `sales_order` (question 6), `privacy_request`, `admin_audit_event` (append-only).
- Export: customer self-service JSON download; admin export of a data subject found by email (account plus guest orders plus mail records).
- Erasure: customer self-service account deletion (password re-entry), admin erasure of a subject (account and/or guest orders), anonymisation of order contact fields, removal of mail records.
- Retention job: anonymise the contact fields of closed orders older than a configured period (off until the owner sets it, question 3); purge of old audit and request rows.
- Minimal admin audit log (question 7) and a "Privacy" admin area (requests, lookup, export, erase, audit list) behind a new permission (question 8).
- Storefront: "Your data" account page (download, delete), legal pages (privacy notice, terms, cookies) as i18n placeholder content, footer links, notices at registration and checkout (question 11).
- Guard tests (privacy by design): PII sentinel checks on logs, domain event payloads and stored error codes; cookie and third-party origin inventory; inventory completeness.
- Docs: RoPA template, processor and DPA list, cross-border transfer notes, breach notification procedure outline, data-subject request runbook for the shop owner, cookie inventory.

**Later (each its own plan or step):** guest self-service export and deletion through an emailed link (reuses `customer_auth_token`-style tokens, question 5); marketing consent and a consent log (no marketing mail exists, email plan non-goal); automatic deletion of inactive accounts (question 12); rectification of the email address (no email change route exists for customers or admins); restriction of processing as a status; per-request identity verification flows; erasure requests forwarded to carriers or the bank through their APIs; encrypted backups and backup retention (no production deployment exists yet); a cookie consent manager (only if a client adds non-essential cookies, question 9).

**Non-goals:** legal conclusions; client-specific legal text in master (AGENTS.md section 15); analytics, tracking or consent banners for third-party scripts (there are none); deleting orders, payments, refunds or shipments (accounting records; ADR 0017); deleting staff accounts (admins are deactivated, ADR 0009).

## Existing functionality found (verified 2026-10-29 on `origin/main` at `b6421e0`)

### What already protects personal data
- **Logs:** the problem filter logs only method and path of an unhandled error, never query strings or bodies ("query strings and bodies may contain personal data or secrets", `apps/api/src/common/problem.filter.ts` lines 146-151); `summarizeError` logs class names and codes, never messages (`apps/api/src/common/error-summary.ts`); the API has no HTTP access log of its own (no `morgan`, `pino` or request logger in `apps/api/src`).
- **Log-capture specs exist per area** (helper `captureLogs` in `apps/api/src/test/shipping-fixtures.ts` line 147), used by `auth-login.spec.ts`, `shipments.spec.ts`, `shipping-checkout.spec.ts`, `shipping-providers.service.spec.ts`, `track-shipments.job.spec.ts`, `send-emails.job.spec.ts`, `email-sender.spec.ts`, `email-admin.spec.ts`, `order-mail.handler.spec.ts`, `payments-create.spec.ts`, `payments-webhook.spec.ts`, `payments-reconcile.spec.ts`, `payments-races.spec.ts`, and the provider fixtures of `resend` and `nova-post`. Each asserts its own area; there is no shared sentinel list or cross-cutting guard.
- **Events carry ids only:** `domain_event` payloads are `{orderId, orderNumber, status}` style (`docs/contracts/events.md` lines 13-23; `apps/api/src/database/schema/domain-events.ts` comment "never personal data"); `last_error_code` is limited to a code pattern so messages cannot be stored (ADR 0019).
- **No IP address or user agent stored** for customers or admins (`schema/customers.ts`, `schema/admin-auth.ts` header comments; ADR 0016 "Privacy").
- **Mail outbox minimisation:** no rendered body, raw token or guest link stored; codes only; purged after 30 days (`failed`, `unknown`: 90) by job 10, which also deletes `customer_auth_token` rows 30 days after expiry (`docs/api/jobs.md` line 34; `schema/email.ts` lines 19-36).
- **Media:** uploads are re-encoded without EXIF (GPS included), XMP, IPTC or ICC (ADR 0028 line 38).
- **Guest and reset tokens** live in URL fragments, never in logs or `Referer` (ADR 0023; `apps/storefront/next.config.ts` `TOKEN_PAGE_SOURCE`, `ACCOUNT_EMAIL_PAGE_SOURCE`).
- **Storefront CSP:** `default-src 'self'`, `script-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'` (plus `'unsafe-eval'` in development), `connect-src 'self'`, `img-src 'self' data:` plus the configured image hosts (`apps/storefront/next.config.ts` lines 85-111). No third-party script, font or analytics origin; no `next/font/google`, `gtag` or analytics import found in `apps/storefront/src` or `apps/admin/src`.
- **Roles:** orders and customers are personal data, so the viewer gets neither; owner and manager get `orders.*`, `customers.*`, `shipping.manage`, `email.manage` (`packages/core/src/auth/roles.ts` lines 18-37).
- **Card data:** the shop never sees card data; `payment` keeps the card system and the last four digits only (`schema/payments.ts` lines 89-95; `docs/dependencies.md` line 166).

### What exists for deletion and retention
- Customers are **deactivated, never deleted** (ADR 0016 "Privacy", ADR 0017 "Retention"); `PATCH /admin/customers/{id}` sets `isActive` and deletes the sessions (`docs/api/customers.md` line 56). No route deletes a customer, an order or an admin user.
- **Foreign keys are ready for a customer delete** (ADR 0019 "What happens when a row elsewhere is deleted"): `customer_session`, `cart` (and its lines), `customer_auth_token` cascade; `sales_order.customer_id` and `email_message.customer_id` become NULL. The order keeps its contact snapshot.
- **Orders cannot be deleted once they have a shipment or a payment** (`shipment.order_id` and `payment.order_id` are `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, `schema/shipping.ts` line 287, `schema/payments.ts` line 139), and the order item, adjustment and status journal triggers allow only the cascade path (ADR 0019).
- **`sales_order` contact columns are NOT NULL with CHECKs** (`schema/orders.ts` lines 71-76, 114-125): email lower-case 3 to 254 characters, phone `^\+?[0-9]{7,15}$`, names 1 to 100 characters. Anonymisation must either use values that pass these CHECKs or change them (question 6).
- **`sales_order` is not immutable** (HANDOFF "Header immutability" gap): contact fields can be UPDATEd today, which an anonymisation needs.
- **Retention jobs today** (`docs/api/jobs.md`): job 4 deletes dispatched events after 7 days; job 5 expired carts (`CART_TTL_DAYS`, default 30); job 6 customer sessions at their absolute end (`CUSTOMER_SESSION_MAX_HOURS`, default 2 160 h = 90 days); `ExpiredSessionCleanup` deletes expired admin sessions (`apps/api/src/auth/session-cleanup.ts`); job 7 abandoned uploads after 24 h; job 10 mail and auth tokens (above). Orders and customers: no automatic deletion.

### What exists for access and audit
- **Journals with an admin actor (writes only, not reads):** `order_status_change.actor_admin_id`, `shipment_status_change.actor_admin_id`, `shipment.created_by`, `payment_event.actor_admin_id`, `payment_refund.actor_admin_id`, `stock_movement.actor_id`, `shipping_provider_setting.updated_by`, `media_upload.created_by`, `email_message.created_by_admin_id`. All `SET NULL` on admin delete.
- **No audit log** for customer deactivation, unlock, admin user changes, or any read of personal data (HANDOFF Next steps 4 "audit log"). Admin login success logs the user id only (`apps/api/src/auth/auth.service.ts` line 95).
- **Admin search by email:** `GET /admin/orders?q=` (exact order number or exact email), `GET /admin/customers?q=` (start of the email), `GET /admin/email/messages?recipient=` (substring) (`docs/api/orders.md` line 114, `docs/api/customers.md` line 54, `docs/api/email.md`). These put an email address into a URL query string (risk R2 below).

### Customer-facing routes that an export can reuse
`GET /store/account/me`, `GET /store/account/orders` and the order detail (`docs/api/customers.md`, `docs/api/orders.md`); the admin order view behind `OrderReadPort` (`packages/core/src/orders/read-port.ts`) already assembles contact, lines, shipping, shipments and payments.

### What is missing
A data inventory and its guard; export; erasure and anonymisation; a retention rule for orders; an audit log; a privacy request record; legal page placeholders; the cookie inventory written down; a RoPA template; a processor list that includes Nova Post (only Resend and Monobank are entered as processors in `docs/dependencies.md` lines 159-166; the shipping plan section 10 promised the carrier entry to this plan); a breach procedure.

"Today" is what the code does now; "Proposed" is this plan (subject to questions 2, 3 and 12). Legal bases are proposals for the lawyer: contract performance (C), legal obligation (L), legitimate interest (LI).

## Proposal

### 1. Architecture and boundaries
| Piece | Where | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| Pure rules: `decideErasure(facts)` (blockers and the action per order), the anonymisation plan as data (which column becomes what), `buildPersonalDataExport(facts)` (versioned document `fluxkit.personal-data-export/v1`), `retentionCutoff(now, days)`, privacy request statuses and transitions, `requestDueAt(receivedAt)` | `packages/core/src/privacy/` (TDD) | destructive and legally relevant decisions belong in tested pure code; core stays I/O-free |
| Data inventory registry (`table.column -> {class, export, erasure, retention}`) and its guard specs | `apps/api/src/privacy/inventory/` | it must see the Drizzle schema; a new column without an entry fails CI |
| Privacy module: subject lookup, export service, erasure service, request records, retention job 12, audit writer | `apps/api/src/privacy/`, `apps/api/src/jobs/` | core module, no feature flag: erasure must reach every core table; features join through the inventory contract |
| Store routes (export, delete account) | `apps/api/src/customers/account/` (owned by the customers module, calls the privacy services) | the account surface already lives there |
| Admin routes (requests, lookup, export, erase, audit) | `apps/api/src/privacy/admin/` | new permission (question 8) |
| Admin area "Privacy" | `apps/admin/src/areas/privacy/` | ADR 0027 pattern |
| Storefront "Your data" page, legal pages, notices | `apps/storefront/src/app/[locale]/(site)/account/data/`, `.../legal/[page]/`, messages `uk.json`, `en.json` | ADR 0026; texts are i18n content, replaceable per client fork |
| Privacy documents | `docs/privacy/` (new folder) | one place for the owner and the lawyer |

**Feature modules:** the inventory is a public contract for features (an `inventory` export next to `featureModule`, or a documented rule that a feature's tables are listed in its README and in a registry the feature contributes; decided in the ADR). With a feature deleted its entries disappear with it (feature-matrix `deleted` stays green).

### 2. Export (data portability and access)
- **Document:** JSON, UTF-8, `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="personal-data-<date>.json"`, `Cache-Control: no-store`. Top level `{format: "fluxkit.personal-data-export/v1", generatedAt, subject: {kind: "customer" | "email"}, account?, orders[], mail[], sessions[], cart?, processors[]}`. `account`: email, names, phone, created, last login, verified, active. `orders[]`: number, status, dates, locale, contact snapshot, addresses, lines (product and variant titles in the order's locale and all stored locales, SKU, quantity, amounts), totals, shipping method and pickup point, shipments (carrier, tracking number, status, dates), payments (status, card system, last four, refunds). `mail[]`: template key, status, created and sent times (no body exists). `sessions[]`: created, last seen, expiry (no IP exists). `processors[]`: the names and roles from configuration (Resend when `EMAIL_PROVIDER=resend`, each loaded carrier and payment provider) so the subject sees who received data.
- **Never exported:** password and token hashes, `request_fingerprint`, idempotency keys, provider ids that only the shop can use (`provider_ref`, `provider_payment_id`), admin ids and admin free text that names staff (D12, D15, D21: reasons are exported for the subject's own order; admin ids are not).
- **Customer self-service** (question 14): `GET /store/account/data-export` (customer session, no-store, rate limit `dataExport` 5 per 24 h per customer). Synchronous: a customer's data is small; the order list is bounded (a hard cap of 10 000 orders, beyond which the route answers `409 rule_violation export_too_large` and the shop owner exports from the admin).
- **Admin:** export of a subject found by email (account, guest orders with that `sales_order.email`, mail rows with that `to_address`), recorded on a `privacy_request` and in the audit log.
- **Guest buyers** (question 5): through the shop owner in v1 (the owner checks the person controls the address, for example by replying to a mail from it, and records how in the request note, without copying documents).

### 3. Erasure and anonymisation (red zone: destructive, irreversible; needs explicit owner approval of the rules)
**Blockers (core `decideErasure`)**: an order `awaiting_payment`; an order `paid` and not `fulfilled` or `cancelled`; a payment attempt live or `create_unknown`, or a refund not final; a shipment that is active (`pending`, `created`, `in_transit`, `arrived`, `returning`, `unknown`). With a blocker the self-service deletion answers `409 rule_violation erasure_blocked` with the reasons (`open_order`, `payment_in_progress`, `refund_in_progress`, `active_shipment`), and the admin sees them; nothing is changed. The admin may not override a blocker in v1.

**Account deletion (one transaction, customer row locked first, then orders by id order, then mail rows):**
1. Re-check the blockers under the locks.
2. Anonymise the contact fields of the customer's orders per question 2 (recommended: at once for closed orders, which every remaining order is after step 1).
3. DELETE `email_message` rows with `customer_id` = the customer or `to_address` = the account email, except rows `sending` under a live lease (they are cancelled by the next step 7 rule: the deletion waits up to the 2-minute lease or answers `409 erasure_blocked mail_sending`; decided in step 7).
4. DELETE the `customer` row (sessions, cart, auth tokens cascade; `sales_order.customer_id` SET NULL).
5. Insert a `privacy_request` (`kind = erasure`, `subject_kind = customer`, `status = completed`, counts of what was changed; no email) and an `admin_audit_event` when an admin did it.
6. Commit; clear the session cookie.

**Order contact anonymisation** (core plan, applied by SQL in one statement per order): email, phone, names per question 6; `shipping_address` and `billing_address` reduced to `{countryCode}`; `cancellation_reason` NULL; `access_token_hash` and `access_expires_at` NULL (the guest link stops working); `request_fingerprint` replaced; `contact_erased_at` set. Kept: number, status, dates, locale, currency, amounts, lines, adjustments, shipping snapshot, shipments, payments, refunds, journals. The order mail handler and the shipment create refuse an erased order (`no_recipient`, `contact_erased`).

**Guest orders** (no account): the admin erases by email (all orders with that `sales_order.email`), with the same blockers and steps 2, 3 and 5.

**Retention job 12 `anonymise-orders`** (question 3): when `ORDER_CONTACT_RETENTION_DAYS` is set, anonymise closed orders whose final date (`fulfilled_at`, `cancelled_at`, `expired_at`, or the last refund) is older than the period, in pages of 100 inside the 30 s budget, one short transaction per order, skipping any with a blocker. Unset (default) = off, as today, with one startup warning in production ("order contact data is kept without a retention period"). The same job purges `privacy_request` and `admin_audit_event` rows past their retention (step 4 row).

**What erasure cannot reach (documented in the runbook):** data already at processors (the shop asks Resend, Nova Post and Monobank under their terms), backups (they age out under the host's retention; restoring a backup must re-apply erasures: the `privacy_request` rows list erased order numbers and customer ids, never contact data, so they can be re-applied), proxy logs (L3).

### 7. Security
- **Re-authentication:** account deletion needs the current password (scrypt check outside the transaction, `verifyDummy` timing as login), CSRF header, rate limit `accountDelete` 5 per hour per customer and per address; a wrong password is `400 validation_failed path password code invalid` (no lockout increment beyond the login rule: decided in step 7).
- **Export:** customer session only (the guest token never exports: a guest link is a bearer link to one order, not to a person's data); admin export behind the new permission, audited, rate limited `privacyExport` 30 per hour per admin.
- **Lookups by email:** admin privacy lookup is `POST /admin/privacy/lookup {email}` (body, never a query string), so it never reaches proxy logs (L3).
- **No PII in new logs, events, codes or audit rows:** the sentinel guard (step 2) covers the new routes and job 12.
- **Erasure is irreversible:** typed confirmation in both UIs; a preview (counts and blockers, `POST /admin/privacy/requests/{id}/erase-preview`) before the real call; the real call is idempotent per request id (a repeat answers the stored result).
- **Default deny:** every new admin route needs the new permission; every store route needs the session; route-walk specs as for other areas.
- **Backups and restore:** documented re-application of erasures from `privacy_request.result` (section 3).

## Risks
- **R1 Irreversible deletion of the wrong data:** an erasure bug destroys contact data that accounting or a dispute needs. Mitigated by core TDD, blockers, preview and typed confirmation, idempotent requests, the review, and (question 2) keeping order totals and lines.
- **R2 Personal data in edge proxy logs:** admin list routes put emails in query strings (`?q=`, `?recipient=`); nginx and Caddy log full URLs by default. The API is clean; the deployment is not under our control. Mitigation per question 13.
- **R3 Legal periods unknown:** retention, required order fields and transfer mechanisms are owner and lawyer decisions; the template ships with retention off, which is the opposite of minimisation until the owner sets it.
- **R4 Data at processors and in backups is out of reach** of the erasure; documented, with re-application after restore.
- **R5 Inventory drift:** a later column or feature table stores personal data unnoticed; mitigated by the classification guard (it fails CI) and the feature inventory contract.
- **R6 Account takeover becomes account destruction:** a stolen session could delete an account; mitigated by the password re-entry and the rate limit (no two-factor exists).
- **R7 Admin free text** (D10, D12, D15, D21, `privacy_request.note`) may hold personal data typed by staff; the append-only journals cannot be erased. Mitigated by UI hints only (a rule, not a guarantee).
- **R8 Migration nullability:** making four `sales_order` columns nullable touches every reader (`OrderReadPort`, mail handlers, shipment create, admin views, api-client types); a missed reader crashes on an erased order. Mitigated by the type change (nullable in TypeScript) and the erased-order fixtures in step 7.
- **R9 Blockers can be permanent** (a shipment stuck `unknown`, a refund `unknown`): such a subject cannot be erased until an admin settles them; the admin view names the blocking object.
- **R10 The legal placeholder guard** could block a client release that intentionally keeps a sentence; the marker is a precise string, documented.
